A school where every child is seen

Every child who walks through our gate is gifted in some way. Our job is to help them find out how.

At Peniel, pupils grow academically, socially, emotionally and morally, guided by teachers who care about character as much as grades. Bright classrooms, a creative curriculum and practical life skills help curiosity turn into a lifelong love of learning.

How Peniel grew. It began in 2011 as Peniel Children Play Centre, a crèche started by Mr and Mrs Ojo. Playgroup, nursery and basic education followed, and today the school welcomes pupils through to secondary level.

Questions parents ask

Which classes do you offer?

Crèche, Playgroup, Pre-Nursery, Nursery, Primary (basic education) and Secondary. Ask the admissions team which class fits your child’s age.

Which curriculum do you follow?

Peniel is a dual-curriculum school. Nigerian and British standards are taught side by side.

What are the school hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 4:00pm.

How do I apply?

Collect and complete the enrollment form, meet the admissions team for a review and simple assessment, wait for the admission decision, then submit documents and attend orientation. Use the form above to start on WhatsApp.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the class. Message or call the admissions office for the current fee schedule.

Can I visit the school first?

Yes, and we encourage it. Book a campus tour by calling 0803 788 0700 or messaging us on WhatsApp.
